# Sales staff guide

## Lead-to-customer workflow

1. Review new demo requests in CRM and assign an owner promptly.
2. Confirm organisation, contact details, product interest, expected users, decision process, and follow-up date.
3. Move the demo to **scheduled** only after agreeing a date; the prospect receives the scheduled notification.
4. After the session, mark it **completed**, record outcomes/objections as notes, and set the next action.
5. Use pipeline statuses consistently: new → contacted → qualified → demo scheduled → demo completed → trial/quotation sent → negotiating → won/lost.
6. Convert the lead once the organisation is commercially qualified. Conversion reuses lead data and product interest; do not create a duplicate customer manually.

## Quotations

Select the correct customer, product, and plan. Describe scope, implementation, quantity, discounts, tax, expiry, and terms clearly. Browser totals are ignored; the backend calculates the commercial total. Preview before sending. A customer decision must use the signed quotation link. Only accepted quotations can become invoices.

## Data discipline

Use activity history for factual events and notes for context. Never store passwords, payment-card data, API tokens, or license keys in CRM notes. Mark lost opportunities with a useful reason. Do not promise demo capabilities, discounts, integrations, delivery dates, or entitlements that are not present in the approved product/plan records.

## Handoff to finance and onboarding

After acceptance, convert the quotation and notify finance. Confirm the billing contact and tax details. Subscription/license activation occurs only after verified payment. Once converted to a customer, coordinate identity verification and portal linking with an administrator.
