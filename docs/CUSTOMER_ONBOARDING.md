# Customer onboarding guide

## Before access

Your KernelBridge contact confirms the organisation, primary contact, selected product/plan, quotation, invoice, and payment. Create your login at `https://kernelbridge.com/register` using your business email. Verify the email when requested. KernelBridge staff will verify your authority and link the login to the organisation account.

## Portal

After linking, the dashboard shows your organisation number, subscriptions, licenses, quotations, invoices, payment history, and support tickets. Signed document links expire for security; reload the portal to generate a fresh link. Contact support if an expected organisation or record is missing—do not create repeated accounts.

## Product activation

KernelBridge supplies a license key through an approved secure channel after the matching invoice is paid and the subscription is active. Enter it only in the official product activation screen. The product communicates server-to-server with KernelBridge; never paste the key into email, chat, screenshots, or public tickets.

## Renewal

Renewal reminders are sent before expiry. Confirm plan, billing details, and purchase approval early. Renewal takes effect only after the matching renewal invoice is paid. During an approved grace period the product may remain available, but it should not be treated as a substitute for renewal.

## Support

Open the customer portal, choose **Support**, select the product/category/priority, describe expected and actual behavior, and attach safe screenshots or documents. Do not include passwords, full license keys, patient/student confidential data, payment-card data, or API credentials. Reply within the ticket so its history and SLA remain complete.
